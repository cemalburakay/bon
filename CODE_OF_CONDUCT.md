# Code of Conduct

Bon aims to be a respectful, useful space for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Share only information you have permission to disclose; redact private prompts, credentials, and personal data from reports.
- Accept that maintainers may set scope, close duplicate or out-of-scope discussions, and ask for changes to keep collaboration safe and focused.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, hateful or discriminatory conduct, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private data, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will review reports, may remove or edit harmful content, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently block participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private contact/reporting options; do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers will handle reports as discreetly as practical, but cannot promise a particular response time or outcome.

This is a project-specific code of conduct written for Bon. The community guidelines provide expectations for participation; this file is not a promise of formal mediation or legal process.
